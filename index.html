Method

  • Daily values are averaged into monthly means (a month needs at least 8 daily values).
  • The yearly cycle is removed by subtracting the average of each calendar month.
  • A straight line is fitted (least squares). The 95% interval is widened if neighbouring months are correlated.
  • A trend is shown only for 3 years or more. We do not say “statistically significant”: that needs a proper test.

Limitations

  • Total-column ozone is not surface ozone.
  • One place (one 0.25° grid cell). Clouds, snow and low sun can add error.
  • The exact product version is still to be confirmed in NASA documentation.
  • O3-NET-1 is an independent concept. It is not an official NASA project, not endorsed by NASA, and not a launched satellite. It is not a health warning or an ozone-hole detector.
